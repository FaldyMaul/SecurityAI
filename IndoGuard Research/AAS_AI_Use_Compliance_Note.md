# AAS AI Use Compliance Note

Purpose: keep the Australia Awards application process honest while still using research support productively.

Based on `AI_rules.md`, AI may help with:

- Understanding the application questions.
- Brainstorming high-level ideas.
- Organising thoughts before the applicant writes.
- Checking spelling and grammar on text written by the applicant.
- Improving understanding of the topic and evidence.

AI must not:

- Generate final answers to AAS application questions.
- Rewrite, improve, paraphrase, or polish the applicant's answers.
- Create a reintegration plan or attached application document on behalf of the applicant.
- Produce text that is then adjusted to look original.

Therefore, the files I created are not final application essays. They are research notes, positioning material, evidence, and a drafting workbook. The applicant should write the final answers personally, using their own words, examples, and speaking style.

Recommended workflow:

1. Read `AAS_Research_Evidence_Bank.md`.
2. Read `AAS_Idea_Positioning_and_Roadmap.md`.
3. Use `AAS_Essay_Drafting_Workbook.md` to draft each answer in your own words.
4. Only after you write your own draft, use AI or a human reviewer only for allowed support such as grammar checks, clarity questions, or source verification.

Important note for authenticity:

The benchmark PDF is useful for understanding structure, specificity, measurable goals, and problem-solution flow. It should not be copied in wording, rhythm, examples, or claims.
