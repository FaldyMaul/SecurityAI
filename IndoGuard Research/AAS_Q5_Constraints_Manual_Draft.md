Question:

List any possible constraints you think may prevent you from achieving above tasks.

## Purpose of This Answer

The panel needs to see that your plan is ambitious but realistic.

## Constraint 1: Limited High-Quality Indonesian Safety Data

Possible mitigation:

- Start with formal Indonesian, informal Indonesian, and Indo-English.
- Use human verification.
- Add one regional-language proof of concept only if native-speaker annotation is feasible.
- Avoid blind machine translation.

## Constraint 2: False Positives, Over-Blocking, and Censorship Concerns

Possible mitigation:

- Treat the model as risk triage, not automatic censorship.
- Route ambiguous cases to human review.
- Keep taxonomy transparent.
- Measure false positives and over-blocking.
- Anchor governance in UNESCO, OECD, and NIST principles.

## Constraint 3: Adoption Barriers in Government or SOEs

Possible mitigation:

- Start with controlled Telkom sandbox testing.
- Produce evidence before proposing wider adoption.
- Engage legal, security, product, and policy stakeholders early.
- Use alumni and academic networks to build trust.

## Constraint 4: Compute and Operational Cost

Possible mitigation:

- Begin with compact classifiers or adapted open models.
- Focus on evaluation first.
- Prioritise high-risk categories.
- Use existing sandbox infrastructure where possible.

## Constraint 5: Sustaining Collaboration

Possible mitigation:

- Keep collaboration grounded in specific outputs: benchmark, white paper, training, review workshop.
- Maintain Australian academic and AAS alumni relationships.
- Connect the work to Indonesia-Australia cyber and critical technology cooperation.
