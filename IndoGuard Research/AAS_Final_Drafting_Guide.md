## Final Positioning

Use this refined idea consistently:

Indonesian AI Safety Evaluation Framework for Sovereign AI Infrastructure

Meaning:

- A research-led framework to evaluate AI safety failures in Indonesian-language generative AI systems.
- Focused on formal Bahasa Indonesia, informal Bahasa Indonesia, and Indo-English code-switching.
- One regional language may be included only as a proof of concept if human verification is feasible.
- Long-term purpose: support safer AI deployment in public institutions, SOEs, and essential digital services.

Avoid drifting back to:

- A generic national AI inference platform.
- A corporate MVP.
- A Telkom product roadmap.
- A claim that the model will solve misinformation.
- A claim that no Indonesian AI safety work exists.
