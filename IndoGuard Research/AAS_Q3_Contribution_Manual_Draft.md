# AAS Q3 Manual Draft: Contribution to Change or Reform

Do not submit this file. Write the final answer manually in your own words.

Question:

How have you contributed to solving a challenge or implementing change or reform in the context of one of the AAI priority development areas?

Include:

- Leadership knowledge, skills, and practice you consider well established and effective.
- People or organisations you worked with.
- Creative methods used.

## Purpose of This Answer

The panel needs proof that you have already led change, not only planned future impact.

## Best Example Options

Choose one main example.

Option A: BP BUMN semantic matching system

- Best for fairness, objective decision-making, AI for institutional reform.
- Result: 35 percent improvement in enterprise talent mapping accuracy.
- Public-good angle: reduced subjectivity and strengthened institutional decision-making.

Option B: PDP Law Task Force

- Best for cyber security, privacy, governance, and citizen protection.
- Public-good angle: stronger institutional responsibility over personal data.

Option C: WAD Workload Analysis and Talent Management System

- Best for AI leadership and institutional transformation.
- Result: INAICA 2025 Operational Excellence Award.
- Public-good angle: evidence that you can lead AI adoption responsibly at scale.

## Recommended Structure

1. Situation:
   What was the challenge?

2. Your role:
   What responsibility did you take personally?

3. People and organisations:
   Who did you work with?

4. Creative method:
   How did you make the change possible? Examples: workshops, translating AI concepts, prototypes, stakeholder alignment, iterative testing, mentoring.

5. Result:
   Use one clear measurable outcome.

6. Reflection:
   What did this teach you about leading responsible AI change?

## Leadership Skills To Show

- Influencing non-technical stakeholders.
- Building trust.
- Turning ambiguity into structured action.
- Mentoring others.
- Balancing innovation with governance.
- Delivering measurable institutional improvement.
