## H. Better Problem Framing for Indonesian Llama Guard

### Do Not Lead With This

Avoid making the main problem:

> Indonesia has many phishing incidents and fraud losses, therefore I need to build a localized LLM Guard.

This bridge is too weak because phishing/fraud statistics show a cybercrime environment, not a specific LLM safety failure.

### Lead With This Instead

Use this problem:

> As Indonesian institutions begin using generative AI in customer service, public information, internal knowledge management, and decision support, they need a way to test whether AI prompts and outputs are safe in Indonesian language and local context. Global safeguards such as Llama Guard 4 are useful, but their multilingual coverage and evaluation are still not enough for Indonesia-specific deployment. Existing research such as IndoSafety shows that Indonesian-centric LLMs can still produce unsafe outputs, especially in colloquial and local-language settings. This creates an urgent governance gap for SOEs and public institutions that want to deploy AI responsibly.

### Why This Is Stronger

This framing directly supports the Indonesian Llama Guard idea:

1. Institutions are adopting generative AI.
2. LLM systems need input and output safety classifiers.
3. Llama Guard 4 is a useful global model, but Indonesian coverage is not the core tested strength.
4. Indonesian language, slang, code-switching, and local cultural issues create safety blind spots.
5. A localized guardrail and benchmark can help institutions test AI before deployment.
6. This contributes to Strong Institutions, cyber resilience, responsible digital transformation, and public trust.

### Where Fraud Can Still Fit

Fraud should be a supporting example, not the main evidence.

Use this bridge only if needed:

> Fraud and phishing are examples of content categories that a safety classifier may need to detect, especially when prompts ask an AI system to generate impersonation scripts, fake customer-service messages, credential-harvesting templates, or social-engineering content.
