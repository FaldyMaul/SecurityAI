## H. Technical Language Guide

The current answers are mostly understandable, but reviewers may not be technical specialists. Use plain definitions where possible.

| Term | Reviewer-friendly wording |
|---|---|
| AgentLab | A low-code drag-and-drop AI workflow platform that helps employees build AI tools without starting from zero. |
| ModelHub | A secure internal gateway that connects AI applications to approved company-controlled or sovereign AI models. |
| Llama Guard-style system | An AI safety checker that reviews user instructions and AI answers, then flags risky content for human review. |
| Prompt | A user's instruction or question to an AI system. |
| Output | The AI system's answer. |
| Sovereign LLM | An AI model operated through controlled local or institutional infrastructure, reducing reliance on public platforms. |
| False positive | When a safety checker wrongly flags safe content as risky. |
| Over-blocking | When a safety system blocks too much and becomes unfair or censorship-like. |
