## K. Sentences To Improve

1. Weak: "This is urgent."

   Better: "This is urgent because AI-scaled fraud could affect citizens before Indonesian institutions have reliable local-language safety evaluation tools."

2. Weak: "RMIT is my second choice because..."

   Better: "After comparing UTS and RMIT, I placed UTS first because its NLP sub-major, ethics subject, and project stream align more directly with my goal of building Indonesian-language AI safety evaluation."

3. Weak: "This study will move my career from AI platform engineering to AI cyber security leadership."

   Better: "This study will equip me to move from building AI platforms to leading responsible AI and cyber security evaluation for Indonesian institutions."

4. Weak: "For my community, this study can help protect citizens..."

   Better: "For Indonesian citizens who rely on digital public and financial services, this study can help reduce exposure to AI-assisted fraud, unsafe automation, and weak data protection."

5. Weak: "I worked with stakeholders who understood the organisation..."

   Better: "I worked with BP BUMN human capital stakeholders and technical colleagues, combining their institutional knowledge with my AI architecture and evaluation role."

6. Weak: "The creative leadership challenge was trust."

   Better: "The most important leadership challenge was building trust among non-technical users who needed to understand when AI recommendations were reliable and when human judgement should override them."

7. Weak: "Each task supports the next: evidence, capability, then adoption."

   Better: "Together, the tasks create a realistic pathway: first evidence, then practitioner capability, then wider institutional adoption."

8. Weak: "while keeping public benefit above institutional pride or corporate interest."

   Better: "while framing the work as shared public value, not only as one organisation's internal product."
