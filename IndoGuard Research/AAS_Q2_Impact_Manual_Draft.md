# AAS Q2 Manual Draft: Impact on Career, Life, and Community

Do not submit this file. Write the final answer manually in your own words.

Question:

What impact do you believe this study will have on your career, life and community?

## Purpose of This Answer

The panel needs to see:

- Career impact: how the study changes your capability and role.
- Personal impact: how the study changes your judgement, confidence, and responsibility.
- Community impact: how Indonesians benefit.
- Why the impact is realistic.

## Recommended Structure

1. Career:
   Explain that the degree will help you move from AI platform engineering to responsible AI safety evaluation and governance.

2. Life:
   Explain that you have grown through self-learning, but now need academic discipline and international exposure.

3. Community:
   Explain the public benefit: safer AI-enabled services, stronger institutional trust, reduced AI-assisted fraud risk, better privacy protection, and more accountable deployment.

4. Practical outputs:
   Mention the Indonesian AI safety evaluation framework, benchmark, research prototype, and training program.

5. Measurable targets:
   Choose only realistic targets you can defend.

## Target Options

Use only the ones you can honestly defend:

- Build a research prototype and evaluation benchmark within 12 months after return.
- Test at least five high-priority safety categories.
- Focus first on formal Indonesian, informal Indonesian, and Indo-English.
- Train 500 engineers, product managers, and public-sector stakeholders within three years.
- Engage at least three institutions for review, validation, or collaboration.

## Public-Good Reminder

Do not write as if the main benefit is Telkom's product growth. Telkom is the testing and deployment vehicle. The community benefit is safer Indonesian AI deployment.
