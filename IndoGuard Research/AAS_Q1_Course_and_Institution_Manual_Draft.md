# AAS Q1 Manual Draft: Course and Institution

Do not submit this file. Write the final answer manually in your own words.

Question:

Why did you choose your proposed course and institution? Please include information on efforts you have undertaken so far to obtain information in your study option.

## Purpose of This Answer

The panel needs to see:

- Why UTS Master of Artificial Intelligence is your first preference.
- Why the course is necessary for your specific public-good mission.
- What research you have done before choosing UTS and RMIT.
- Why your current experience is not enough without formal academic study.

## Recommended Structure

1. Human trigger:
   Write one short, true moment from your AI Sandbox, AI Playground, PDP, or LLM work.

2. National problem:
   Explain that Indonesia needs safe generative AI deployment, especially in Bahasa Indonesia, informal language, and code-switching.

3. Technical gap:
   Explain that global and regional guardrails exist, but Indonesia still needs rigorous national evaluation and governance.

4. UTS fit:
   Connect your need to UTS subjects and structure.

5. Study research:
   Mention what you reviewed: UTS course, RMIT course, AAS priority fields, Australia-Indonesia development partnership, AI safety research, Llama Guard, SEA-Guard, IndoSafety.

6. Why now:
   Explain why your current role creates urgency and responsibility.

## Evidence Options

Use only 2-3, not all:

- BSSN 2024: 330.5 million cyber anomalies and 26.7 million phishing activities.
- OJK IASC: IDR2.6 trillion reported fraud losses by 23 May 2025.
- Llama Guard 3-1B: Indonesian not listed as a supported language.
- IndoSafety: formal, colloquial, and local-language Indonesian safety evaluation is an active research frontier.
- AAS priority: Strong Institutions, Cyber security, Misinformation and disinformation, Research and innovation.

- The Ethics of Data and AI.
