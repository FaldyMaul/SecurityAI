I will share sandbox findings with national cyber and digital agencies such as BSSN and Komdigi, while engaging Australian academics and AAS alumni for peer review. Where possible, I will invite feedback through review workshops so the benchmark reflects both Indonesian realities and international practice. The output will be a practical benchmark report and guidance note for safer AI deployment in SOEs and public services. This links my return plan to Australia-Indonesia cooperation on cyber security and critical technology, while creating a realistic pathway: first evidence, then practitioner capability, then wider institutional adoption.
